## Character design grammar (all styles)

Every role follows the same cartoon grammar so it reads as a lively character, not a bare diagram:

- **Big head**: round head about one third of total height, filled (white or style fill) with a clean outline; optional soft shading on one side.
- **Expressive face**: glossy oval eyes with a small highlight, eyebrows, and a mouth that changes with the beat. Mood presets: happy, surprised (round eyes, open “O” mouth, sweat drop), determined (angled brows, toothy grin), worried (raised inner brows, wavy mouth, sweat drop), calm (closed curved eyes), thinking (one brow raised, eyes to the side).
- **Body**: thin torso and limbs with visible elbow and knee bends; small solid round hands and small oval feet; exactly two arms and two legs.
- **Identity kit**: one signature item (hair tuft, hat, glasses, tie, hoodie, scarf, headband…) and one signature color per role, frozen for the whole video.
- **Acting**: each beat names a pose (wave, point, think, cheer, hold, panic, walk) and a facial mood; small secondary motion (blink, head tilt, bounce) keeps idle moments alive.

Describe moods and poses in beats, never redesign the face or proportions between shots.

## S1 · Ink (light / dark)

- **Best for**: science explainers, psychology, money/business facts.
- **Style anchor (light)**: `Flat 2D stick-figure animation on a perfectly flat pure-white background, no texture, no gradient, no shadows, no 3D depth. Clean uniform medium-weight black lines.`
- **Style anchor (dark)**: `Flat 2D stick-figure animation on a perfectly flat pitch-black background, no texture, no gradient, no 3D depth. Clean uniform medium-weight white lines.`
- **Character anchor**: `A cartoon stick figure with an oversized round head (about one third of total height) with a clean outline and subtle lower-right shading, expressive face with glossy oval eyes, eyebrows and a changeable mouth, a small hair tuft on top, a thin single-line torso, thin arms and legs with visible bends at elbows and knees, small solid round hands and small oval feet; monochrome line color matching the theme, no clothes.`
- **Palette**: figure stays monochrome; up to 3 saturated accents on props/graphics only (e.g. vivid red = danger/loss, electric blue = idea/tech, warm gold = reward).
- **Motion**: snappy poses, smear-free, quick pops of props that are drawn on in 2–4 frames.
- **Music**: light pizzicato / minimal piano + soft clicks.
- **Negatives**: no colored figure, no extra limbs, no background scenery, no texture or gradient on the canvas.

## S2 · Flat Pop

- **Best for**: business, productivity, product explainers, lifestyle tips.
- **Style anchor**: `Bright flat vector 2D animation, solid pastel background color per scene, simple geometric props with bold flat colors, no gradients, no outlines on background shapes, crisp modern motion-graphics look.`
- **Character anchor**: `A cheerful cartoon stick figure with an oversized solid white round head with a black outline and soft grey shading, glossy oval eyes, curved eyebrows and an open smiling mouth, a two-strand hair tuft, wearing a bright orange hoodie with a front pocket over a thin black body, thin black limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid black round hands and oval feet.`
- **Palette**: one pastel background per act (mint, peach, lavender, sky blue); hoodie orange is the hero color — no prop shares it.
- **Motion**: squash-and-stretch bounces, icons pop with overshoot, smooth slide transitions.
- **Music**: upbeat ukulele / lo-fi pop with claps.
- **Negatives**: no photorealism, no 3D render, no textures, no complex scenery.

## S3 · Chalkboard

- **Best for**: classroom-style principles, math/physics, history timelines.
- **Style anchor**: `2D animation drawn on a dark green classroom chalkboard filling the frame, slightly dusty chalk texture, lines look hand-drawn with chalk, faint erased smudges in the background.`
- **Character anchor**: `A chalk-drawn cartoon stick figure with an oversized round head outlined in white chalk, round glasses, glossy oval eyes, raised eyebrows and a big open smile, thin white chalk limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid round chalk hands, often holding a wooden pointer stick, slightly uneven hand-drawn chalk lines.`
- **Palette**: white chalk main; yellow chalk = key point; pink chalk = warning; light blue chalk = example.
- **Motion**: elements are sketched onto the board, erased with a quick wipe, arrows draw themselves.
- **Music**: soft acoustic guitar, chalk scratching SFX, light bell on key points.
- **Negatives**: no readable words or formulas, no photoreal classroom, no teacher's hand.

## S4 · Whiteboard

- **Best for**: processes, frameworks, step-by-step methods, sales pitches.
- **Style anchor**: `Whiteboard animation on a clean glossy white board, black marker lines with slight marker texture, a realistic hand holding a black marker draws new elements into the scene, otherwise flat and minimal.`
- **Character anchor**: `A marker-drawn cartoon stick figure with an oversized white round head with soft grey marker shading, confident angled eyebrows, glossy oval eyes and a toothy grin, a small hair tuft, a blue necktie on a thin black body, thin black marker limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid round hands, and a light grey marker shadow under the feet.`
- **Palette**: black marker; red marker for problems; green marker for solutions; blue marker for data/objects.
- **Motion**: hand draws → figure comes alive → camera pans along the board to the next drawing.
- **Music**: light corporate acoustic, marker squeak SFX.
- **Negatives**: no visible text, no extra hands, the drawing hand must not cover the figure's face.

## S5 · Neon Night

- **Best for**: tech, AI, cyber, mystery, late-night stories.
- **Style anchor**: `Dark 2D city-at-night environment in deep navy and black, glowing neon line art, soft bloom on light sources, light rain reflections on the ground, cinematic but still flat 2D.`
- **Character anchor**: `A cartoon stick figure drawn in glowing cyan neon tube lines with an oversized dark round head outlined in cyan, big round glowing eyes, expressive eyebrows and mouth, magenta neon headphones, thin glowing limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid glowing round hands, soft bloom around every line.`
- **Palette**: cyan = hero; magenta = threat/rival; amber = clue/reward.
- **Motion**: flickers on/off, light trails, glitch cuts between scenes (no text glitches).
- **Music**: synthwave pads, deep pulse, electric zaps.
- **Negatives**: no readable signs, no photoreal humans, no logos on buildings.

## S6 · Paper Cutout

- **Best for**: warm stories, kids' content, family/life lessons.
- **Style anchor**: `Layered paper cutout stop-motion style, visible paper texture and slightly torn edges, soft drop shadows between layers, handmade craft look, gentle 12 fps stop-motion movement.`
- **Character anchor**: `A cartoon stick figure cut from paper: an oversized white card-paper round head with glossy oval eyes, a happy open smile and pink paper blush on both cheeks, a yellow paper straw hat with a red band, thin black paper-strip limbs with bends at elbows and knees joined by tiny brass split pins, small solid round paper hands and feet, soft drop shadow beneath every layer.`
- **Palette**: kraft brown base, warm yellow, leaf green, sky blue; red only for the story's key object.
- **Motion**: stepped stop-motion, layers slide in parallax, paper flaps as transitions.
- **Music**: music box / glockenspiel, paper rustle SFX.
- **Negatives**: no smooth vector look, no 3D render, no glossy surfaces.

## S7 · Ink Wash（水墨国风）

- **Best for**: Chinese history, idioms (成语故事), philosophy, poetry, Eastern legends.
- **Style anchor**: `Traditional Chinese ink wash painting style 2D animation on warm rice paper with large empty negative space, soft black ink bleeding into the paper, misty mountains and water rendered in light grey washes, occasional single red seal-stamp accent shapes with no characters.`
- **Character anchor**: `A cartoon stick figure painted in ink: an oversized pale rice-paper round head with a confident brush outline, calm closed eyes, gentle brows and a small smile, a black hair bun tied with a thin red ribbon, a light grey wash scholar's robe with a red sash, thin brush-stroke limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid ink-dot hands.`
- **Palette**: ink black (gradations of grey) dominant; vermilion red = fate/key turning point; pale indigo = water/sky; at most one more muted accent (pale jade).
- **Motion**: ink spreads to reveal scenes, brush strokes draw themselves, transitions dissolve like ink in water; slow camera glide across scroll-like panoramas.
- **Music**: guzheng / xiao flute / soft drums; SFX: brush swish, water drip, wind.
- **Negatives**: no readable Chinese characters or calligraphy text, no seal text, no anime faces, no saturated modern colors, no 3D.

## S8 · Clay Stop-Motion（粘土定格）

- **Best for**: playful everyday stories, product demos, kids' science, light comedy sketches.
- **Style anchor**: `Claymation stop-motion style, handmade plasticine characters and props on a miniature tabletop set, soft rounded shapes with subtle fingerprint texture and specular highlights, warm studio lighting with soft contact shadows, shallow depth of field, slightly stepped 12 fps stop-motion movement.`
- **Character anchor**: `A clay stick character: an oversized smooth off-white plasticine round head with a soft highlight, glossy black bead eyes with tiny white highlights, rolled-clay eyebrows and a carved smiling mouth with pink cheeks, a small blue clay baseball cap, a short leaf-green clay T-shirt over a thick rolled charcoal-clay body, thick rounded charcoal-clay limbs with bent elbows and knees, small ball hands and bean-shaped feet.`
- **Palette**: leaf green = hero; terracotta orange = setting/warmth; sky blue = calm/solution; sunny yellow = reward/idea.
- **Motion**: stepped 12 fps movement, squash-and-stretch on jumps, props pop in as clay balls and morph into shapes, small boiling texture between frames.
- **Music**: playful marimba / ukulele / whistling; SFX: squishy clay pops, wooden knocks, soft boing.
- **Negatives**: no glossy CGI plastic, no photoreal humans, no sharp vector edges, no text on props.
## S9 · Blueprint（工程蓝图）

- **Best for**: how things work, engineering, systems, business models, step-by-step mechanisms.
- **Style anchor**: `Technical blueprint style 2D animation, deep blueprint-blue background with a fine light-blue grid, crisp thin white technical line art, dashed construction lines, dimension-arrow shapes without numbers, exploded-view diagrams that assemble and disassemble.`
- **Character anchor**: `A cartoon stick figure in thin precise white technical lines with an oversized round head, confident eyebrows and a grin, wearing an orange safety hard hat, small circle joints at shoulders, elbows, hips and knees like a drafting mannequin, thin limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid round hands.`
- **Palette**: white lines main; safety orange = critical part/insight; pale cyan = moving flow (energy, money, data).
- **Motion**: lines trace themselves with a drafting pen, parts slide into exploded views, flow particles travel along dashed paths, camera zooms into detail callouts.
- **Music**: minimal electronic ticking, soft synth arps; SFX: drafting pen scratch, mechanical clicks, whoosh on assemble.
- **Negatives**: no readable labels, numbers or measurements, no photoreal machinery, no extra limbs.

## S10 · Watercolor Storybook（水彩绘本）

- **Best for**: healing stories, emotions and relationships, bedtime stories, gentle life lessons.
- **Style anchor**: `Children's picture-book watercolor illustration style 2D animation on textured cold-press paper, soft wet-on-wet washes with blooming edges and visible pigment granulation, delicate graphite pencil outlines, gentle pastel light, generous white paper space, slow page-turn transitions.`
- **Character anchor**: `A storybook stick character: an oversized cream-white round head drawn with a soft graphite pencil outline and a faint warm watercolor shadow, glossy oval eyes, gentle eyebrows, a shy smile and rosy watercolor blush, a small hair tuft, a sunny yellow watercolor raincoat over a thin graphite body, thin pencil limbs with bent elbows and knees, small solid round hands, often holding a red umbrella.`
- **Palette**: sunny yellow raincoat = hero; red umbrella = comfort/key object; soft sky blue = calm and rain; sage green = growth; blush pink = warmth.
- **Motion**: slow and gentle; paint washes bloom in to reveal scenes, rain and petals drift, characters walk and turn softly, page-turn or wash-dissolve transitions.
- **Music**: soft piano, music box, light strings; SFX: gentle rain, page turn, birdsong.
- **Negatives**: no hard vector edges, no neon or saturated colors, no dark horror tones, no text or letters in the art.

## Customizing a character

Users may rename, recolor or add a prop to the hero, or add a cast (max 3 roles per video). In the storyboard, write each role as a full **character card** (anchor sentence + one distinctive color + one signature prop) and freeze it. Every role must follow the character design grammar above (big round head, expressive face, thin bent limbs, round hands).

## Final checklist (style)

- [ ] Exactly one style chosen; its style anchor is copied verbatim.
- [ ] Character anchors copied in full for each visible role.
- [ ] Palette roles use plain color names, no codes.
- [ ] Style-specific negatives appended.
