# Storyboard Template (Phase A)

Present the storyboard in the user's language. Stop at the end and ask for approval.

## Narrative arc: Hook → Tension → Breakdown → Payoff

| Act | Share of runtime | Job |
|-----|------------------|-----|
| **Hook 钩子** | first 3–5 s (≈ 10 %) | A surprising question, paradox or striking image. Never open with greetings or definitions. |
| **Tension 冲突** | ≈ 25 % | What most people believe or do, and why it fails. Raise stakes. |
| **Breakdown 拆解** | ≈ 45 % | The mechanism, steps or story turns, one idea per shot, each shown by an action. |
| **Payoff 落点** | ≈ 20 % | The takeaway in one memorable line, then a call to action or open question (stories may end on a visual punchline instead). |

Narration pacing: Chinese ≈ 4–5 characters/s, English ≈ 2.3–2.7 words/s. Leave ~0.5 s breathing room at each shot end.

Rewrite rules: keep the source's claims, names and numbers; cut repetition; expand thin sources with an example or analogy; never invent data, quotes, studies or product claims. Mark anything uncertain as `[需核实 / verify]`.

## Output format

### 1. Header

- **标题 / Title** (+ one alternative)
- **核心观点 / Core message** (one sentence)
- **规格 / Spec**: ratio · duration · `N` shots × shot length · generator
- **画风 / Style**: ID + name (+ light/dark for S1)
- **调色板角色 / Palette roles**: plain color names and what each means
- **配乐 / Music**: genre, energy curve across the 4 acts
- **旁白 / Narrator**: full voice profile from `voice-profile.md`

### 2. Character cards

One block per role (max 3): name, full anchor sentence (from the style catalog or customized), distinctive color, signature prop.

### 3. Shot table

| # | Time | Act | Narration (exact) | Visual action (2–3 beats) | Camera / layout | Transition out |
|---|------|-----|-------------------|---------------------------|-----------------|----------------|

- Every shot has a concrete action and at least one visual change every 2–3 s.
- `Transition out` names what links to the next shot: a matched action, a carried prop, screen direction, or a deliberate cut.

### 4. Full narration

The complete narration as one block (for recording or TTS), with character/word count and estimated duration.

### 5. Approval question

End with something like: “确认这个分镜吗？确认后我会输出每个镜头的视频提示词；也可以告诉我要改哪里。” Then stop.

## Final checklist (storyboard)

- [ ] Ratio, style, duration, generator and narration language stated.
- [ ] Shot count = duration / shot length; narration fits the time budget.
- [ ] Hook lands in the first 3–5 s.
- [ ] Every shot has action, change every 2–3 s, and a transition link.
- [ ] No invented facts; uncertain items flagged.
- [ ] Ends with an approval question and no prompts yet.
