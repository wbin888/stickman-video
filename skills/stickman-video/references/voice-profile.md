# Voice Profile

Pick one narrator per video and freeze it. A scene, style or mood change never recasts the narrator; emotion is carried by visuals and music, not by changing the voice.

## Fields (all required)

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Identity & age | adult male, early 30s |
| Language & accent | Mandarin Chinese, standard neutral accent |
| Timbre | warm, slightly husky |
| Register | mid-low |
| Delivery | conversational storyteller, confident, light smile |
| Pace | medium-fast, crisp pauses at sentence ends |
| Recording | close-mic, dry studio, no reverb, no music bleed |

Expand short user wishes (“女声”, “deep male voice”) into all fields and show them in the storyboard.

## Frozen voice block

Render the profile as one English sentence and paste it verbatim into every prompt, e.g.:

`Narrator voice: adult male in his early 30s speaking standard Mandarin Chinese, warm slightly husky mid-low timbre, conversational confident storyteller delivery with a light smile, medium-fast pace with crisp pauses, close-mic dry studio recording with no reverb.`

## Suggestions by content

- Science / business: calm, clear, mid register.
- Mystery / neon: low, slower, intimate.
- Kids / paper cutout: bright, friendly, slightly higher.
- Ink wash / history: steady, measured, storyteller.
- Comic / pixel: energetic, punchy, faster.

## TTS tip

If the generator's built-in audio is inconsistent, recommend generating silent clips and recording one continuous narration (human or TTS) laid over the edit. Mention this in the audio note.
